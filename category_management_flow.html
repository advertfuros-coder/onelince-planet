⚖️ Merchandising Power Comparison

🛒 Basic Shopify

Hierarchy: Flat collections. Manual nesting via menus only.

Fields: Generic Metafields. Manual setup for every new category.

Fees: Unified global transaction fee across all items.

🌍 Online Planet

Hierarchy: Native 3-level tree with auto-path indexing for speed.

Fields: Inherited Attribute Templates for auto-form generation.

Fees: Category-specific commissions (e.g. 3% Tech vs 12% Fashion).
