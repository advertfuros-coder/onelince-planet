⚖️ Inventory Capability Comparison

🛒 Basic Shopify / Woo

Alerts: Simple "Below X" threshold alerts. No sales velocity context.

Priorities: All low-stock items treated equally in the dashboard.

Resolution: Email notification sent. Restock requires manual login and order.

🌍 Online Planet

Alerts: Predictive 7-day stock-out forecast with seasonal adjustment.

Priorities: 3-tier risk classification based on SKU revenue contribution.

Resolution: Automated Restock Trigger with direct supplier orchestration.
