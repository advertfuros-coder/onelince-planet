⚖️ Promotion Capability Comparison

🛒 Basic WooCommerce

Targeting: Broad, mostly global or by simple category.

Usage Check: Basic total limit. Limited per-user protection.

Insights: "How many times used" — no ROI calculation.

🌍 Online Planet

Targeting: Hyper-segmented (New Users, Premium, Specific SKUs).

Usage Check: Multi-layered (Total, Per-User, Min Purchase, Max Discount).

Insights: Integrated Analytics (Revenue vs. Discount Cost tracking).
