⚖️ Merchandising Capability Comparison

🛒 Basic Shopify Theme

Banners: Hard-coded or theme settings. Limited design control per-device.

Featurings: Global collections only. Manual removal of "Old News."

Tracking: Relies 100% on Google Analytics or Pixel apps.

🌍 Online Planet

Banners: Dynamic JSON-driven canvas. Full style & alignment control per banner.

Featurings: Section-based targeting with temporal logic (Auto-expiry).

Tracking: Native internal attribution engine for real-time ROI monitoring.
